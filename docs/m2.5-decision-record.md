# Decision record: Milestone 2.5 research reset

- **Date:** 2026-10-03
- **Status:** decided
- **Scope:** the temporal-agency thesis (NORTH_STAR.md), the temporal
  contestant (Tesseract), and the planned scenario families.
- **Supersedes:** nothing. EXPERIMENT.md and NORTH_STAR.md are unchanged and
  frozen.

## Decision

**Disposition A: Stop.** The temporal-architecture program stops on
value-of-information grounds:
- no Tesseract implementation;
- no new scenario families;
- no Milestone 3 in its planned form.

What the decision rests on:
- **Coverage.** Every concept and mechanism in NORTH_STAR has shipped or
  published prior art (research-landscape-2026-10.md §4;
  capability-matrix.md §4).
- **No strong residual hypothesis.** The only hypothesis on the thesis that
  all three judges kept (H2) is medium cost, and its mechanism predicts a
  tie (residual-hypotheses.md §3).
- **Attribution.** A win against the frozen §7 baseline could not be
  credited to time-indexing without register, dependency/TMS and
  typed-intention arms (residual-hypotheses.md §6.1).

## What this decision is not

- **It is not an EXPERIMENT.md §13 kill.** §13 was never run, and no
  real-model contestant has run on any scenario
  (`results/milestone-2/README.md`).
- **It does not claim that temporal navigation was shown not to help.**
  Whether time-indexed access helps an agent reopen and remediate its
  decisions remains untested anywhere found.
- **It does not judge the infrastructure.** The harness, evaluator, runtime
  and model gateway are sound and reusable.

## Re-open triggers

Any of these reopens the question. H2 (residual-hypotheses.md §3.1) is then
the first experiment, with its kill rule as recorded.

- **(a)** A matched result on decision reopening or remediation in which a
  time-indexed arm beats the better of a decision-register arm and a
  dependency/TMS arm, with a confidence interval excluding 0.
- **(b)** Whole-state temporal navigation beats a baseline that already has a
  register, TMS and a bitemporal store.
- **(c)** Full-text evidence that dependency tracking fails exactly where
  time-indexing succeeds.
- **(d)** Evidence that detecting implicit links improves with time-indexed
  state rather than with relevance inference or re-check policies.

## Parked (not authorized)

- **H2:** bitemporal decision premises for class-D retroactive facts.
- **H3:** prevented-future mis-learning in outcome-feedback memories. It is
  off-objective, a reliability note only.

## If the work continues anyway

The only honest form of the original intuition is a separate,
measurement-first program on **commitment standing under late information**
(NORTH_STAR_V2_PROPOSAL.md §6). Its research question: which minimal
representation (current state, dependency-tracked commitments or
time-indexed state) is needed in which regime, at what cost.

The program would need its own novelty check against STALE, ClawArena, TWIST,
StateMemBench and MerchantBench. It would reuse released benchmarks before
building new ones (benchmark-reuse-assessment.md §5). It is not Milestone 3
of this project.
